# S-607 Paid tier decision (a document, not code)

| | |
|---|---|
| Status | READY after 60 days of traffic |
| Size | S |
| Difficulty | Expert |
| Phase | 5 Scale and monetize |
| Needs | Site live 60 days; S-403 on; ideally S-602 shipped for at least 30 days; the owner's numbers |

## Goal
Decide, from evidence, whether to stay free with a sponsor, add a paid watchlist, or sell a data API.
Produce a decision record with a recommendation and the smallest paid experiment that would test it.
**The go or no-go is the owner's.**

## Inputs the owner provides (if one is missing, write "not provided"; never estimate)
- Monthly visitors and the share of visits to `/watchlist/`, `/check/`, `/data/`.
- Inbound messages from employers, attorneys or prospective sponsors (count and a one-line summary each, no names).
- Feed and email subscriber counts.
- Running costs per month (domain, hosting, email provider).
- Hours the owner spends per month on upkeep.

## Steps
1. Create `docs/decisions/paid-tier-<YYYY-MM>.md` with these sections:
   - **Evidence** — the numbers above in one table, each with its source and date.
   - **Options**

     | Option | What is sold | What it needs built | Running cost | Legal or trust risk |
     |---|---|---|---|---|
     | A. Free + one sponsor | the S-601 slot | nothing new | none | low |
     | B. Paid watchlist | saved lists across devices, a monthly emailed report of who became current, team sharing | accounts, payments, storage of client data, S-501/S-502 | payments fees, storage, support time | holds other people's case data: needs a privacy policy rewrite, security review and terms of service |
     | C. Data API | stable, versioned JSON with an uptime promise and a commercial licence | S-609, API keys, usage limits | low | low; data is public domain, so the value is reliability only |
     | D. Donations | nothing | a donate link | none | low |
   - **What the evidence says** — for each option, which numbers support or weaken it. Be explicit when the sample is too small.
   - **Recommendation** — one option, one paragraph.
   - **Smallest experiment** — something that takes less than a day to build and produces a yes/no signal in 30 days
     (for example: a "Team features — tell us what you need" link on `/watchlist/` to the feedback form, counted; or a
     priced "request access" page with no payment taken). No payment processing is built in an experiment.
   - **What would change the recommendation** — two or three thresholds.
2. Add a row to `docs/DECISIONS.md` (next free D-number) marked **PROPOSED — awaiting owner**, linking to the file.
3. Log it under "Owner actions": "Read `docs/decisions/paid-tier-….md` and decide."

## Acceptance
- [ ] The decision file exists with every section; each number has a source or is marked "not provided".
- [ ] Four options compared, including the cost and the trust risk of holding client data.
- [ ] One recommendation and one experiment that needs under a day of work and no payment processing.
- [ ] Recorded in DECISIONS as proposed; nothing built; nothing priced publicly.

## Pitfalls
- Do not propose attorney-referral fees or lead sales (decisions D26, O5).
- A paid tier must never put the core data, the checker or history behind a paywall ("Non-goals" in `docs/MVP.md`).
- Do not invent market sizes or conversion rates. If a number is needed and unknown, say what would measure it.
