## Goal
Visa numbers reset each fiscal year (October to September), so "how did FY 2024 go for EB-3 India?" is a
natural question and a search people make. Build `/fiscal-year/<year>/` for every fiscal year with at least
ten verified months, plus an index page. Past tense only.

## Definitions
- Fiscal year `Y` = periods `(Y−1)-10` … `Y-09` (`fiscalYearOf` in `src/lib/period.js`).
- For each line × area (Final Action Dates) within the year:
  `start` = the cut-off in the first month of the year that has data; `end` = the last month with data;
  `net` = `describeChange(end, start)`; `monthsBack` = months whose change from the previous month (which may be
  September of the prior year) is a retrogression; `monthsUnavailable` = months whose value is `Unavailable`;
  `monthsCurrent` = months whose value is `Current`.

## Files to touch
`src/lib/fiscalYear.js` (new, pure), `test/fiscalYear.test.js` (new), `src/site/pages.js` or `src/site/fiscalYearPages.js`
(new), `src/site/format.js` (`urls.fiscalYears`, `urls.fiscalYear(year)`), `scripts/build-site.mjs`, `test/site.test.js`.

## Steps
1. `src/lib/fiscalYear.js`: `fiscalYearPeriods(year)` → 12 periods; `fiscalYearStats(points, year)` → the object above
   (input: `dataset.series(...)`); `eligibleFiscalYears(dataset)` → years with ≥ 10 verified months, ascending.
2. Tests: a full year; a year with a gap; a retrogression in October measured against the previous September;
   Current and Unavailable months; a year with nine months (not eligible).
3. `fiscalYearPage({ site, dataset, year })`:
   - kicker "Fiscal year", `<h1>Visa Bulletin in fiscal year <Y></h1>`, lede "October <Y−1> to September <Y>:
     where each cut-off started, where it ended, and how often it moved back. `<n>` of 12 months are in our data."
   - one table per group (employment, family): rows = lines, columns = the five areas; each cell shows
     `start → end` and the net movement chip; cells link to the line × area page;
   - a second table per group "Months it moved back / was Unavailable" with counts;
   - one chart per group for the **All Chargeability** area: series = that group's lines limited to the first five
     (`renderCutoffChart` supports five colours), points filtered to the year; with the sentence "This is what happened in
     the year, not a forecast.";
   - links to each month's bulletin page, and previous/next fiscal year.
4. `fiscalYearsIndexPage`: a list of eligible years, newest first, each with its month count; a sentence explaining the
   fiscal year. Link to the index from the archive page (each "Fiscal year <Y>" heading links to its page when eligible).
5. Register all pages in `build-site.mjs` (they then enter the sitemap automatically). `npm run check`.

## Tests to add (`test/site.test.js`)
- A page for an eligible year renders with the right `<h1>` and contains "not a forecast".
- The index lists exactly `eligibleFiscalYears(dataset)`.
- No fiscal-year page contains `will` followed by a verb about dates: assert it does not match
  `/\b(will|expected to|likely to)\b/i`.

## Acceptance
- [ ] `/fiscal-year/<year>/` exists for each fiscal year with at least ten verified months, and nowhere else.
- [ ] Each shows start, end, net movement, months moved back and months unavailable per line and area.
- [ ] One chart per group; links to every month; an index page; all in the sitemap.
- [ ] Wording describes the past only (test).
- [ ] `npm run check` green; two years checked at 375 and 1280.

## Pitfalls
- The current fiscal year is incomplete: it qualifies only once it has ten months, which is intended.
- A net change between `Current` and a date has no day count; show the label ("Cut-off imposed", "Became current").
- EB-5 lines changed shape over time; `dataset.series` already follows lineage. Do not special-case it here.
