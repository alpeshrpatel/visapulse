# Growth plan: getting people to VisaPulse and bringing them back

Written 2026-11-16. This is the plan after the launch kit (`docs/LAUNCH.md` covers the launch gate and first posts).
It says who we are trying to reach, how they will find us, why they will return, and what to do each week.

| [`SEO.md`](SEO.md) | Which page answers which search, on-page rules, link earning, technical checklist |

**Who does what.** Agents build pages and tools (the Phase 7 stories in `docs/STORIES.md`) and draft text.
**Only the owner** posts, emails, signs up for accounts, spends money, or speaks for the project in public.

**Read this first: what is known and what is assumed.** No traffic exists yet, and no paid keyword tool was used.
Statements below about audience size, where people gather and what they search are working assumptions drawn from the
strategy pass. Each is marked *(assumption)* where it matters. The first job of measurement (S-403, S-409, S-410) is to
replace them with our own numbers.

## 1. Rules for all promotion (these protect the one thing we sell: trust)

1. **Never promote before the launch gate in `docs/LAUNCH.md` is met.** A first visit that sees "unverified" everywhere is wasted.
2. **Never post a number that is not verified** against the official page. On bulletin day, speed matters, accuracy matters more.
3. **No predictions, no wait-time estimates, no "your date will be current by…".** Not in a post, a reply, a title or an ad.
4. **Always say you built it.** No second accounts, no asking friends to post as strangers, no fake reviews or testimonials.
5. **Answer first, link second.** In a community, the number and the official link come before any link to us.
6. **Never post in a thread about someone's personal crisis** (a denial, a deadline, a family separation).
7. **No unsolicited direct messages. No scraped or bought email lists.** One-to-one emails to people whose address is public for business contact only (`OUTREACH.md`).
8. **No paid advertising** until the brand question is settled (DECISIONS O3) and there is revenue or a stated budget from the owner.
9. **No paid links, link swaps or referral fees.** (Attorney referral fees also raise legal-ethics problems: DECISIONS D26, O5.)
10. **Corrections are public.** If someone finds an error, thank them in the thread, fix it, list it in the changelog (S-608).

## 3. How growth is supposed to work: five loops

A loop is something that brings a visitor **and** makes the next visitor more likely. Each loop names the stories that build it.

| # | Loop | How it turns | Built by |
|---|------|--------------|----------|
| 1 | **Search pages** | A page answers one search exactly → it ranks → visitors arrive → some link or share → it ranks better | Done: month pages, category × area pages. Next: S-804, S-803, S-801, S-806, S-407, then S-802, S-805 |
| 2 | **Bulletin day** | New bulletin → we publish verified tables fast → posts and searches bring a spike → visitors follow the feed or sign up → they come back next month without us posting | S-302, S-406, S-404, S-504, S-810; later S-501…S-503 |
| 3 | **Group-chat sharing** | A visitor copies two lines of text with the numbers and a link into a WhatsApp or Telegram group → several people in the same line click → one of them shares it next month | S-307, S-807, S-404 |
| 4 | **Embeds and citations** | An attorney or blogger embeds the card or cites a page → a permanent, relevant link → more search traffic and referral visits | S-606, S-808, S-609, `OUTREACH.md` |
| 5 | **Teams** | One HR person uses the watchlist → tells employees to check their date here → employees share in groups | S-602, LinkedIn posts |

Loops 1 and 2 need no one's permission and cost nothing: they come first. Loop 3 is cheap to build and fits how this
audience talks. Loops 4 and 5 need outreach and are slower.

## 6. The monthly rhythm (every month after launch)

The bulletin for month M+1 appears during month M. Call its publication day **B**.

| Day | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| B − 7 to B | `/next-visa-bulletin/` (S-806) carries the "not yet" answer. Nothing is posted. Do not speculate about the date. | – |
| B, hour 0–1 | Data pull request merged after the three-cell spot check (RUNBOOKS RB2). Deploy. | Owner |
| B, hour 1–2 | `npm run bulletin:post` → paste into the channels in `CHANNELS.md`. IndexNow dry run then send (S-810). | Owner |
| B to B + 1 | Reply to every question. Fix and credit any correction. | Owner |
| B + 1 | Enter the USCIS chart designation when USCIS posts it (S-303) and redeploy. A short follow-up post is allowed: "USCIS has announced which chart applies." | Owner |
| B + 3 | One LinkedIn post for employers and attorneys. | Owner |
| B + 7 | Fill the month's row in `METRICS.md`. Run `npm run growth:report` (S-809) when a fresh export exists. | Owner or agent |
| Mid-cycle | One evergreen improvement shipped (a story from queue A) and, at most, one outreach batch. | Agent / owner |

## 8. Money

- Default budget: the domain (about $12 a year). Hosting, analytics and search consoles have free tiers *(check the
  chosen provider's current terms; do not assume)*.
- If the owner chooses to spend, the order of usefulness is: (1) a fluent reviewer for the second language;
  (2) an hour of a lawyer's time on the explainer pages and any affiliate idea; (3) nothing on advertising until a
  page converts visitors to subscribers at a rate worth paying for.
- Revenue comes later and in this order (D26): one labeled sponsor slot (S-601) → digest sponsorship → a paid team
  feature or data API only if S-607's evidence supports it.

## 9. What we will not do
Predictions for clicks · "wait time calculators" · pop-ups or interstitial sign-up walls · cookie-based tracking or ad
networks · buying followers, reviews or links · mass posting the same text to many communities · replying to old
threads only to drop a link · scraping forum users or their case data · pretending to be affiliated with any agency.
